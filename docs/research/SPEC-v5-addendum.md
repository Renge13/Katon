## 7. THRESHOLD RECALIBRATION (v5.1 — fixes Amplified over-assignment)
PROBLEM: old symmetric threshold (all elements "dominant" at 1.6x avg) assigned Amplified to
~39% of no-hour charts. Root cause is STRUCTURAL, not a loose knob: the Day Master's own element
is inflated by construction (the day stem IS the DM = guaranteed +1.0, branch usually reinforces).
Measured on 14,610 real no-hour days: self-element mean = 2.00 vs non-self mean = 1.00, chart
avg = 1.20. So the self clears a 1.6x bar ~half the time just by existing. Judging self-dominance
on the same multiplier as other elements was the error. (Confirmed empirically: 4 of 4 real test
charts came back Amplified under the old rule.)

FIX: asymmetric thresholds.
- Amplified (self-element dominant): self >= 2.0x avg   (higher bar — corrects the head start)
- Governed (controller dominant) / Over-fueled (feeder dominant): >= 1.5x avg
- Depleted (feeder missing): feeder <= 0.15
- Balanced: fallback
Priority order unchanged: Governed > Depleted > Amplified > Over-fueled > Balanced.

RESULT (14,610 no-hour days): Balanced ~38% · Amplified ~21% · Governed ~16% · Over-fueled ~13%
· Depleted ~13%. All five meaningfully present; Balanced & Amplified the two leaders.

VALIDATION (known charts hold): Ladang(self 2.7x) & Hujan(2.2x) stay Amplified correctly;
1961 stays Depleted; 2000 stays Over-fueled; 1989 stays Governed; Samudra stays Balanced.
ONE flip: 9 Dec 1996 Pedang (self only 1.7x, near-even spread) Amplified -> Balanced. Correct;
it was a borderline mis-call before.

CAVEAT: 2.0/1.5 is a defensible calibration toward a plausible target, NOT ground truth (BaZi
traditions disagree on strength classification). The STRUCTURAL correction (self needs a higher
bar) is rigorous; the exact multipliers are tunable. Real validation = cold reads: if Balanced-
classified people say "that's me", the threshold is right.

CONSEQUENCES:
1. Balanced is now the LARGEST segment (~38%), not Amplified. Writing priority shifts toward
   Balanced variants. (Currently 1 Balanced written: Samudra.)
2. No locked content breaks (Ladang/Hujan still Amplified, Samudra still Balanced).
3. Pending tester 9 Dec 1996 is now BALANCED (Metal/Pedang) -> needs a Balanced-Pedang reading,
   not the Amplified-Pedang one. Good for coverage (Balanced is the priority state) but it's a
   new write.

## 9. ANTI-AI PROSE RULES + BRIDGE/BODY DOMAIN RULE (v5.1 — from founder line-edit pass)

### 9a. Bridge owns the situation; body owns the pattern (VALIDATED domain-fork rule)
- The BRIDGE is the only place that may name a specific life-situation (waiting / deciding /
  leaving / etc). One domain has up to 3 bridges; an input sub-option selects which fires.
- TEASER through BEAT 7 must be situation-UNIVERSAL: true for any person of that archetype-state
  facing that domain, regardless of their specific circumstance. Write the paid body from the
  ARCHETYPE PATTERN outward, never from one situation inward.
- Test: read each beat against all the domain's bridges. If a beat only fits one bridge, it is
  situation-welded and must be re-pitched up a level. Beat 3 should name the 2-3 underlying causes
  as EQUALS (e.g. Pedang-Karier: decision-not-yours / data-insufficient / cutting-has-a-cost), so
  every bridge's reader finds their case represented without the body picking a default.
- Phase 2 (later, not now): an optional short circumstance-aware paragraph injected into the paid
  tier per sub-option. Multiplies content (situations x archetype-states); build only after the
  universal body is validated as the floor.

### 9b. Anti-AI prose rules (HARD — same tier as no-em-dash; apply to ALL copy, all tiers)
1. **No "not X but Y" as a sentence skeleton.** "bukan X, (tapi/melainkan/justru) Y" reads as
   machine-balanced and is the #1 AI tell in Indonesian. Assert the positive directly; let contrast
   be implied; if you must contrast, vary the shape and never repeat the skeleton. NOTE: reframe
   beats are *semantically* "not weakness, but strength" — keep the MEANING, kill the repeated
   SHAPE. ("bukan berarti X" to dismiss a misreading is acceptable; "bukan X, tapi Y" balance is not.)
2. **Prefer positive constructions over stacked negatives.** "Ketajamanmu terkendali" > "ketajamanmu
   tidak liar". Say what a thing IS, not the two things it isn't.
3. **No literal English-idiom translation.** Examples that failed review: "clear cut" -> NOT "dipotong
   dengan bersih"; use "hitam dan putih / sesimpel itu". "feel alive" -> NOT "paling hidup"; use
   "paling tajam / paling bersemangat". (Reinforces the existing "never translate English clause
   structure" rule with concrete cases.)
4. **Causal cohesion in the river.** Consecutive sentences must advance and connect. Never place two
   true-but-seemingly-contradictory statements side by side ("you feel alive deciding" + "you're
   anxious deciding"). Link them: the strength and the vulnerability are usually the SAME trait seen
   from two sides ("justru karena kamu paling tajam saat memutuskan, momen yang tak bisa diputuskan
   itu yang paling menggangu"). Show that.

## 10. GOLD STANDARD — the reference reading + the voice profile (v5.1, founder-locked)

**Reference artifact:** `katon-balanced-pedang-karier.html` (Balanced Pedang, Karier). This is the
quality bar. When writing or reviewing ANY reading, compare against it. It is the first reading

### 10a. The founder voice profile (apply to ALL copy — this is HOW the brand writes)
Distilled from the founder's own line-edits. These are preferences, not just rules:

1. **Consequence-first ordering.** Lead with the observable result / how she is misread; let the
   reason trail as the reveal. ("kamu hanya tidak mau memutuskan saat keruh, walau sering dianggap
   tidak berperasaan" — the sting lands last.) NOT reason-then-result.
2. **Minimal connective scaffolding.** Cut/avoid "Maka", "Di situ", "Justru karena itu" as
   sentence-openers. Indonesian flows better with a looser join or a hard stop. This is the single
   most consistent founder note.
3. **Vary rhythm; use the fragment and the rhetorical question.** "Ada masalah ruwet? Kamu akan
   temukan ujungnya." Break smooth declaratives for punch. Never an even wall of prose.
4. **Contrast by CONCESSION, not negation.** Preferred shape: concede the misreading, then reveal
   the truth ("Bisa jadi orang menganggapmu dingin, padahal yang sebenarnya..."). NOT "bukan X,
   tapi Y" (banned, §9b) and don't over-rely on "bukan berarti X" either.
5. **Plain, concrete verbs over literary ones.** "menyiksa" not "menguras"; full "menunggu/
   melakukan" in elevated spots, not clipped "nunggu", when the register is the letter-voice.
6. **No metaphor that needs decoding.** A metaphor must land on first read or it is a liability,
   however poetic. Killed examples: "tahu bentuknya sendiri", "garis untuk turun", "menahan pedang
   yang siap turun". Kept (legible): "pedang terhunus yang tidak menemukan apa pun untuk dipotong".
   Test: if the reader pauses to parse "what does that mean", rewrite it.

### 10b. Structure/substance standards confirmed by this reading
- **Free read = maximal recognition, ZERO mechanism.** She finishes feeling seen + told an answer
  exists that she doesn't have (explicit open-loop line, e.g. "kamu sering tidak paham kenapa").
  This IS the conversion engine (curiosity gap): sharp feeling, withheld mechanism, signposted that
  mechanism is knowable. Withhold the ANSWER, never the ACKNOWLEDGMENT.
- **No cross-tier repetition.** Each of free / bridge / paid-beats does a DIFFERENT job. Lists,
  images, and explanations that appear in a paid beat must NOT also appear in the free read.
  Specifically: cause-lists and the signature metaphor live in the paid beat only.
- **Beat 1 must validate something the free read did NOT say.** Pivot from "you're good at X"
  (repeats free) to a new angle, e.g. the unthanked COST of the gift. Shield-down validation has to
  be fresh, not a restatement.
- **Beat 3 opens straight into the reveal** after at most a one-line re-acknowledgment. Do not
  re-summarize her state; the free read already did that.
- Bridge owns situation; teaser→beat 7 universal (§9a). Anti-AI prose rules (§9b) always on.

## 11. DRIVER vs BEHAVIOR — how one reading fits divergent people (load-bearing, all domains)

The problem: people of the SAME archetype-state behave OPPOSITELY (an Amplified Permata can be a
spendthrift OR a hoarder OR an agonizer). Asserting one behavior as her identity kills the cold read
for everyone who does the opposite. But writing "you sometimes do X, sometimes Y" is Barnum mush.

The resolution: **center the reading on the shared DRIVER; let BEHAVIOR appear as a recognizable
range, never as a verdict.**
- The DRIVER is the single underlying mechanism the archetype-state produces. It is the SAME for all
  of them, and it must be SPECIFIC enough to EXCLUDE other archetypes (falsifiable — a different
  archetype reading it should feel WRONG). Example (Amplified Permata, no Fire): "an exacting eye
  with no off-switch — nothing easily meets your standard, and there's no heat to ever say 'this is
  good enough, rest here.'" A spendthrift (chasing the thing that finally measures up), a perfectionist
  (won't settle), and a self-denier (won't spend unless excellent) are the SAME driver, three faces.
- The BEHAVIOR is the surface expression, which DIVERGES per person. Cover it as a SPREAD she can
  locate herself in — especially in Beat 2 (lived scenes): write scenes recognizable HOWEVER the
  driver expresses, so opposite behaviors both find themselves. Never assert "you do X."

The rule, one line: **specific driver, ranged behavior — never vague driver, ranged behavior.**
The first is the moat (precise mechanism, flexible surface). The second is ChatGPT Barnum (vague
mechanism, vibes). Test: would this driver feel WRONG to a different archetype-state? If yes, it's
sharp enough to flex the behavior without going generic.

Why it matters most in Uang/Karier: behavior varies far more there than in Hubungan (money/work
habits diverge wildly; relational patterns less so). These domains REQUIRE driver-level writing.
This is the copy expression of the "calculation = moat" thesis: the engine pins the driver exactly;
that precision is what licenses behavioral flex without Barnum drift.

## 12. SPECIFIC QUESTIONS — what Katon answers and what it must never answer

Users arrive with concrete in-the-moment questions: "beli rumah ini ga ya", "bulan ini closing ga",
"tembak dia sekarang ga", "cabut dari kerjaan ga", "gue cocok di bidang apa". A generalized reading
seems unable to answer these. Resolution — split them by TYPE; only some are ours to touch:

1. **Outcome / prediction questions** ("will I close this month", "will he say yes"): NEVER answer.
   This is fortune-telling — violates "ini bukan ramalan", fatal in Uang. No product layer should
   ever predict an outcome. This is the line that protects the whole positioning.

2. **Decision questions** ("should I buy / leave / commit"): answer at the level of DECISION-DISTORTION,
   never outcome. Katon does NOT say "yes, buy it." It shows how her archetype-state pattern is
   FOGGING the decision, and gives her a rule to cut through it. Example (Amplified Permata, house):
   "an eye that never finds anything good enough will find flaws in any house — your hesitation is
   your standard refusing to let anything land, not a verdict on this house. The real question isn't
   'is it perfect', it's 'am I waiting for a certainty my eye will never grant?'" That removes the
   distortion so SHE decides. This is BEAT 6 (Cara Memutuskannya) — write it as a distortion-defuser
   aimed at the decision, not a generic life lesson.

3. **Self-knowledge / fit questions** ("what field suits me"): EASIEST, squarely on-brand — it's
   character, not future. Answer freely: what kind of work/relationship/money-relationship fits a
   mind like hers. (Permata: work where discernment IS the product — curation, editing, design, QA.)

**Architecture mapping:** the BRIDGE names her specific situation (her voice); BEAT 6 gives the
decision rule that defuses her specific distortion; teaser→beat 7 stay universal. Phase-2 circumstance
paragraph (§9a) can later add per-situation specificity. NEVER build a per-question answer engine —
that forces either prediction (forbidden) or generic advice (off-brand, unlicensed).

One line: **Katon answers "what's fogging your decision", never "what will happen."** Smaller promise,
honest, and usually what she actually needs — stuck decisions are rarely stuck for lack of a forecast.

## 13. CARD REVAMP — zone-3 repurpose + profile-energy (v5.1, locked)

Resolves the §9c pending decision. The card's emotional arc is NAME (hook) → DIMENSION (inward,
intimate recognition, prose) → ZONE 3 (the hinge) → FEED/DRAIN (outward, social, "which are you").

### 13a. Zone 3 = "How You Land on Others" (replaces the inert Talenta competence-list)
The old "3 talents" zone was a competence list: inert, Barnum, did the least work. REPLACED with the
OUTWARD-FACING identity — what people FEEL / EXPERIENCE around her, how she is PERCEIVED. This:
- bridges inward (dimension) → social (feed/drain): takes the private self and turns it outward.
- is slightly EXPOSING (perception, incl. the gap between intent and impact) → earns a reaction, not a nod.
- is shareable ("omg this is how people see me").
Label suggestion: "Yang Orang Rasakan" / "Cara Kamu Terbaca" (founder picks final string).
Content = 3 short behavioral-perception lines, e.g. (Amplified Permata): "orang ngerasa diukur waktu
deket kamu", "kalau kamu muji, itu beneran berarti", "susah ketebak kamu udah nyaman apa belum".

**HARD BOUNDARY:** zone 3 names HOW SHE'S PERCEIVED, never THE COST she pays for it. "people feel
measured around you" = OK (perception, shareable). "you push people away and you're lonely" = the
WOUND = paid beat 3, never on the card. Perception is shareable; the ache is paid.

### 13b. Profile-energy split (resolves the founder's "police-file" instinct, correctly placed)

prose that unfolds, not bullets that state. Card = scannable identity object; READING = intimate prose.
Never let the reading (paid/free body) drift to profile/bullet format — the prose IS the conversion
mechanism (recognition is a feeling produced by unfolding sentences, not stated by labels).

## 13d. Zone 3 + feed/drain refinement (v5.1 — supersedes 13a's "3 lines")
- **Zone 3 = ONE optional line, never three.** Three bullets of one idea dilutes and tends to restate
  the dimension. Zone 3 is a SINGLE line adding a NEW outward FACET the dimension doesn't cover
  (prefer a FUNCTION fact: how she operates for others, e.g. Governed Matahari = "sought in crisis,
  not celebration"). OPTIONAL: cut it entirely when the dimension already owns the outward story
  (common for inward/Governed states). Never pad to fill the slot.
- **Feed/drain is the card's PRIMARY shareability mechanic** (the only real network loop — TCG lesson:
  rarity/foil/stats don't transfer because there's no collection/competition; the "which are you?
  tag them" social hook does). Make archetype NAMES the visual hero (drives "am I a Gunung?" → friend
  takes the test), drop why-lines as clutter, add a send-prompt ("Siapa Jati/Gunung-mu? Kirim ke dia.").
- **Card completeness ≠ zone count.** A complete card = a realized character (name + modifier +
  dimension flavor + scene) + one social mechanic (feed/drain). Do NOT add zones to feel complete;
  TCG cards are complete as character objects, not full stat sheets. "Is it enough" is an EMPIRICAL
  question answered by the first real shares, not by adding content. Lock good-enough, test, then tune.
