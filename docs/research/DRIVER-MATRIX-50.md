# Katon — 50-Driver Matrix (anti-redundancy logic check)

**Purpose:** one-line driver per archetype-state, derived from MECHANICS, positioned so overlaps are
visible and forced apart. This is a DIFFERENTIATION check (logic), NOT validation (cold read). A driver
passes when it would feel WRONG applied to an adjacent cell (§11 test). Validated cells deepen in
interpreter §3; this matrix is the pre-write guard against redundant narratives.

**How to read a driver:** [the trait] + [what the state does to it]. The archetype fixes the trait;
the state fixes the distortion. Collapse risk is highest (a) across the SAME state for related
archetypes (Yin/Yang pairs), and (b) across states within ONE archetype if the distortions blur.

**Mechanics key per element** (D=self): Wood feeder=Water ctrl=Metal · Fire feeder=Wood ctrl=Water ·
Earth feeder=Fire ctrl=Wood · Metal feeder=Earth ctrl=Fire · Water feeder=Metal ctrl=Earth.

## 甲 JATI — Yang Wood — the upright pioneer (grows up, leads, won't bend)
- **Amplified** [LEAN]: drive with no brake — grows toward the goal so hard it can't stop to ask if the goal still fits, or who got left below.
- **Governed** (Metal floods): the pioneer held back by rules/authority/criticism it can't out-grow — pruned before it can rise, channels upward drive into endurance.
- **Over-fueled** (Water floods): over-watered — so much input/support/possibility it can't choose a direction; potential that never hardens into a trunk.
- **Depleted** (Water missing): growing on no water — pushing upward with no replenishment, ambition running on fumes, stiffening.
- **Balanced** [LEAN]: steady upward growth that doesn't need to announce itself; rooted ambition, the rare one who leads without towering.

## 乙 AKAR — Yin Wood — the adaptive survivor (bends, finds the way around, quietly unbreakable)
- **Amplified**: adapts so fluidly it loses its own shape — always the way around, never the stand; bends to everyone until it can't find what it wanted.
- **Governed** (Metal floods): the flexible one pinned by a hard environment — forced to comply where it would normally maneuver; resilience under constant cutting.
- **Over-fueled** (Water floods): too many options/feelings to flow toward — adaptability becomes drift, every direction watered, none chosen.
- **Depleted** (Water missing): the survivor with nothing feeding the roots — still adapting, but from depletion; clever moves running on empty.
- **Balanced**: the quiet strategist who bends without breaking and keeps its shape; flexible and rooted at once.

## 丙 MATAHARI — Yang Fire — the radiant warm one (lights the room, generous, seen)
- **Amplified**: warmth with no off-switch — gives light to everyone, burns out unseen, needs to be needed.
- **Governed** (Water floods) [WRITTEN, founder chart]: warmth real but gated behind caution; people meet the reserve before the heat and mistake the order for absence.
- **Over-fueled** (Wood floods): too much fuel — flares hot and fast, over-gives, intensity that exhausts itself and others; can't modulate the blaze.
- **Depleted** (Wood missing): radiance with no fuel — warm by nature but nothing feeding it, dims when alone, performs warmth on reserves.
- **Balanced**: steady warmth that lights without burning out; generous and self-sustaining, the rare warm one who also keeps some heat for herself.

## 丁 PELITA — Yin Fire — the inner light (sees what others miss, deep, devoted small flame)
- **Amplified**: the inner flame turned up too high — sees/feels everything, over-burns on others' problems, insight that consumes the self.
- **Governed** (Water floods): the steady small light pressed by doubt/over-thinking — flickers under its own caution; the flame second-guesses its own seeing.
- **Over-fueled** (Wood floods): too much to illuminate — takes on everyone's darkness as fuel, over-empathizes, burns through itself lighting others.
- **Depleted** (Wood missing): the candle with little wax left — gives light it can't replenish, fades quietly before anyone notices.
- **Balanced**: a small steady light that sees clearly without burning out; quiet insight that lasts.

## 戊 GUNUNG — Yang Earth — the immovable anchor (stable, dependable, holds space)
- **Amplified**: so immovable it can't shift even when it should — stability hardened into stuckness; the rock that won't update.
- **Governed** (Wood floods): the steady one constantly pushed/uprooted by others' demands and change — stability under siege, holding while being pried at.
- **Over-fueled** (Fire floods): over-heated foundation — too much input/passion/pressure baked in; the calm one running hot underneath, over-committed to holding.
- **Depleted** (Fire missing): the mountain with no warmth feeding it — holds for everyone, cold and unreplenished, stability with nothing left inside.
- **Balanced**: a calm dependable base that can still move when needed; steady without being stuck.

## 己 LADANG — Yin Earth — the nurturing field (makes others grow, gives, quietly fertile)
- **Amplified** [WRITTEN]: gives until the field is all others' crops and none her own; no fence, so everyone plants in her; giving became safety.
- **Governed** (Wood floods): the giving one overrun by others' needs taking root in her — depleted soil worked by everyone, can't refuse the planting.
- **Over-fueled** (Fire floods): over-warmed field — so much care/input it over-nurtures, smothers, gives past what's wanted.
- **Depleted** (Fire missing): fertile ground with no sun — keeps growing things for others while cold and unfed herself; nurture running on empty.
- **Balanced**: a fertile ground that feeds others and keeps enough for her own harvest; generous with a fence.

## 庚 PEDANG — Yang Metal — the decisive blade (cuts through, decides, high standard)
- **Amplified**: cuts everything, can't sheathe — decisiveness with no restraint, severs what should've been kept, sharpness as first resort.
- **Governed** (Fire floods): the blade under constant heat/pressure — forced to act before it's ready, decisiveness rushed and warped by others' urgency.
- **Over-fueled** (Earth floods): over-resourced blade — too much backing/material, over-prepares, weight dulls the edge; can't cut for over-considering.
- **Depleted** (Earth missing): the blade with no whetstone — still cutting but unsupported, decisions made on reserves, sharpness eroding.
- **Balanced** [WRITTEN]: tegas without harsh, decides cleanly then trusts the call; hesitation = precision, not weakness.

## 辛 PERMATA — Yin Metal — the discerning gem (refined, exacting, values quality/rarity)
- **Amplified** [WRITTEN, Uang]: exacting eye with no off-switch — no Fire to ever say "good enough, rest"; nothing measures up, the eye never rests.
- **Governed** (Fire floods): the gem under heat it didn't choose — refinement pressured/rushed, its standards overridden by others' demands; discernment defended under fire.
- **Over-fueled** (Earth floods): over-buried gem — too much raw material/input, value obscured by accumulation, can't surface the worth for the clutter.
- **Depleted** (Earth missing): the gem with no source rock — refined taste with nothing feeding it, discernment on reserves, polishing what's already thin.
- **Balanced**: refined judgment that knows quality without endless dissatisfaction; high standard that can still rest.

## 壬 SAMUDRA — Yang Water — the vast deep mover (sees patterns, restless, can't be contained)
- **Amplified**: too much water — floods, overwhelms, restlessness with no banks; depth that drowns rather than carries.
- **Governed** (Earth floods): the ocean dammed/bounded by structure it chafes against — vastness forced into channels, movement blocked, depth under containment.
- **Over-fueled** (Metal floods): over-fed source — too much input/intelligence pouring in, over-analyzes, can't stop ingesting to actually move.
- **Depleted** (Metal missing): the sea with no source feeding it — still moving but draining, vision running on reserves, depth thinning.
- **Balanced** [WRITTEN]: calm surface, never-still depth; movement from within not pressure; restlessness is its nature, not a deficit.

## 癸 HUJAN — Yin Water — the soft pervasive rain (feels everything, reads people, nourishes)
- **Amplified** [WRITTEN]: absorbs everyone's feelings involuntarily, no banks; comes home heavy carrying what isn't hers, can't find her own level.
- **Governed** (Earth floods): the rain absorbed/blocked by hard ground — sensitivity pressed down, can't flow or express, feeling trapped under others' solidity.
- **Over-fueled** (Metal floods): over-sourced rain — too much input/perception pouring in, over-reads, soaks up more signal than it can hold.
- **Depleted** (Metal missing): rain with no cloud feeding it — gives nourishment/empathy it can't replenish, pours out until dry.
- **Balanced**: gentle perceptive flow that nourishes without losing itself; feels deeply, keeps its banks.

## COLLAPSE-FLAGS (pairs at risk of redundant narrative — force these apart when writing)

**C1. Depleted, across ALL archetypes — HIGHEST RISK.** Every Depleted driver is "the trait running on
reserves / unreplenished / burning out." Read the 10 above: they rhyme. DIFFERENTIATION RULE: the
Depleted reading must hook on the SPECIFIC missing feeder's flavor, not generic exhaustion — Matahari
missing Wood = "warm but no fuel/reason to shine for"; Permata missing Earth = "taste with no source
to draw new material from"; Samudra missing Metal = "moving with no fresh input/vision feeding it."
The exhaustion is shared; the WHY of the exhaustion is unique. Write the why, never the tiredness.

**C2. Yin/Yang pairs in the SAME state.** Jati vs Akar, Matahari vs Pelita, Gunung vs Ladang, Pedang
vs Permata, Samudra vs Hujan — same element, same state, risk of twin drivers. DIFFERENTIATION RULE:
the Yang member is OUTWARD/active (Jati grows up, Pedang cuts, Samudra sweeps); the Yin is INWARD/
receptive (Akar maneuvers, Permata discerns, Hujan absorbs). The state distortion must land on the
YIN/YANG axis: e.g. Amplified Pedang = cuts too much (action); Amplified Permata = judges too much
(perception). Never let the polarity blur.

**C3. Amplified vs Balanced within an archetype.** Both center the archetype's core trait; risk of
"strong version" vs "very strong version." DIFFERENTIATION RULE: Amplified = the trait WITHOUT
counterweight (extreme, costs something, has an ache); Balanced = the trait WITH self-regulation (the
rare integrated version, no missing piece, ache is subtle/"too composed to be read"). Different
emotional registers, not different intensities.

**C4. Governed vs Depleted within an archetype.** Both can read as "the trait struggling." But Governed
= trait PRESENT but CONTROLLED by an opposing force (gated, pressured, held — the force is external/
present); Depleted = trait UNFED, running down (the lack is internal/absent). Governed Matahari =
warmth gated by water (warmth intact, blocked). Depleted Matahari = warmth with no fuel (warmth itself
thinning). Mechanism opposite; never write both as "the dim sun."

**C5. Over-fueled vs Amplified.** Subtle: both are "too much." Amplified = too much of the SELF
(the trait itself floods). Over-fueled = too much of the FEEDER (the input/support floods, the self
chokes on resource). Amplified Pedang = cuts too much. Over-fueled Pedang = too backed-up/over-prepared
to cut. Different source of the excess.

when its reading is drafted, [VALIDATED] when cold-read passed (interpreter §0 protocol).
