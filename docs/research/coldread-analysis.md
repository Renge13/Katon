# Katon — Cold-Read Walkthrough Analysis

Source: founder's verbatim internal monologue walking through the prototype as a first-time user.
This is worth more than a café test — it captures the exact moments comprehension breaks.
Below: the 30+ individual reactions clustered into ROOT failures, then fixes.

---

## THE HEADLINE FINDING

The copy is not the problem. **Coherence is.** The reading introduces mechanisms it never closes,
uses terms it never defines, and — most damaging — the paid compatibility section CONTRADICTS the
free mirror on its own rules. A user who was tracking carefully caught the engine being inconsistent.
That is fatal, because the entire USP is "accurate, coherent, trustworthy." The moment he thought
"this is starting to not make sense," the product died — not because it was wrong, but because it
FELT wrong, and he couldn't tell which.

The paradox: he understood the mechanics well enough to catch the errors. That means the density
strategy WORKS — he was engaged, tracking, hungry. He churned on incoherence, not on boredom. That's
a fixable failure, and a far better one to have than "I got bored."

---

## ROOT CLUSTER 1 — The reading opens loops it never closes (biggest trust-killer)

Every "here's a mechanism" beat NAMES a thing and then explains something ELSE, leaving the named
thing dangling. He caught all of them:
- "duduk di atas Tikus?? Air lagi? How? Where's this Air come from? it doesn't explain this Tikus" —
  the Spouse-Palace block names the animal and the element, then talks about drowning instead.
- "Where's the bagan?" ×3 — the reading keeps referencing "baganmu" but never shows/defines the chart.
- "what is this mumbo jumbo?" (card) — the card front-loads 8 mechanisms with zero scaffolding.
- "Zheng Guan — is that what it means? charismatic ×2?" — names the god, doesn't land the meaning.

FIX — the **name-it/close-it rule**: if a block introduces a term (Tikus, 子, Zheng Guan, bagan), the
SAME block must close it in one clause. Never name a mechanism you don't immediately cash out.
Not "Kamu duduk di atas Tikus — Air lagi" then silence, but "Kamu duduk di atas Tikus, dan Tikus itu
elemen Air — jadi bahkan 'kursi' pasanganmu pun nambah air ke hidupmu." The mechanism and its payoff
must be welded together, always.

## ROOT CLUSTER 2 — THE COHERENCE CRISIS (the product-killer)

He caught the engine contradicting itself. These are not copy nits — they're logic breaks:

1. **"Water bar is full, why am I weak Fire not strong Water?"** — THE most important miss. The whole
   model hinges on "your DM is the day stem, everything else is measured against it," and we NEVER
   explained that the tallest bar isn't "you." He assumed the dominant element IS him. Every layperson
   will. This single unexplained premise makes the entire strength reading look wrong.

2. **"More water drowns me" then later "Bumi drowns me" — which is it? Is Bumi even in the chart?"**
   The free mirror taught him "Air is your problem." The paid section said "Bumi ngeredupin apimu."
   Both are true in BaZi (Water controls Fire; Earth exhausts Fire) but we taught ONE villain then
   introduced a SECOND without reconciling. He experienced it as the engine forgetting its own story.

3. **"I don't get this year-forecast in my own reading — why does it appear only in the paid couple
   reading?"** — the Luck-Pillar/timing layer appears from nowhere in the paid section. He'd have
   accepted it if his free mirror had a (locked) "cuaca" teaser. It felt bolted on.

4. **"Tarikan kuat but cocok not balanced — how do you measure that? How is that even possible?"** —
   affinity-vs-fit is our BEST concept but we asserted it with no mechanism. He wanted the WHY and got
   a restatement.

FIX — **one villain, named once, carried throughout + a coherence spine.** Pick the dominant drain
(here: Water floods Fire) and make it the through-line. When a second mechanic appears (Earth in
compat), explicitly bridge it: "Ingat apimu lemah karena kebanyakan air? Bumi beda lagi — dia nggak
nenggelamin, dia nyerep. Tapi buat api yang udah lemah, diserep pelan sama capeknya." Same villain
family (things that weaken your fire), now with a named second face, RECONCILED not contradicted.
And: whatever mechanics appear in paid must have a FREE teaser (locked) so nothing is a surprise.

## ROOT CLUSTER 3 — Undefined jargon presented as if obvious

He tripped on terms we treated as self-evident:
- **"bagan"** — uncommon word, used repeatedly, never shown. (He flagged it twice.)
- **"kursi jodoh"** — "weird wording," confusing. It's our translation of Spouse Palace; it doesn't
  carry intuitively.
- **"Yang" in "Yang Api"** — Yang is a common Indonesian relative pronoun. "Api Yang" reads as an
  incomplete sentence ("fire that..."). Real confusion, flagged directly.
- **The kanji 丙 etc.** — "is that the kanji for sun?" He wanted to know what the glyph IS.
- **"Harm (害)"** — "isn't that bad? clashing?" The English metaphysics term alarms without informing.

FIX:
- Kill or gloss "bagan" everywhere → "petamu" / "baganmu (peta lahirmu)" on first use, or just show it.
- Rename "kursi jodoh" → test "sisi pasangan" or "tempat pasangan" or fold into "di dasar dirimu."
- **Yin/Yang problem (he flagged separately):** never write "Api Yang" bare. Options: (a) use the
  quality not the particle — "Api yang menyala terang" (active/expansive) vs "Api yang tenang"; (b)
  use a glossed pair — "Yang (阳, sisi aktif)"; (c) drop yin/yang from the surface entirely and let
  the archetype carry it. RECOMMEND (c) for the mirror, (b) only if a mechanism needs it.
- Glyphs: one-line gloss on first appearance — "丙 (aksara Tionghoa buat 'Matahari Yang')".
- "Harm" → never show the raw English/Chinese term as the headline. Lead with the felt meaning
  ("gesekan halus"), offer the technical name in a de-emphasized chip only.

## ROOT CLUSTER 4 — Mechanism named, IMPACT not delivered (the "so what" gap)

Repeatedly he got the FACT but not what it means FOR him:
- "weak fire = literally weak, can't do everything alone? How to fix?" — wanted the actionable.
- "why do I need more FIRE too?" — favorable-element block explained Wood, skipped Fire.
- "Wibawa ×2 — no positivity here, just pressure/serious/self-pressure. Damn." — we gave only the
  shadow of the Ten God, never the gift.
- "gampang nyalain dia — isn't that generic?" — the impact line was a horoscope platitude.

FIX — **every mechanism block owes BOTH faces + a handle.** Structure each meaning as: (1) the gift,
(2) the cost, (3) something to DO with it. He felt attacked ("no positivity") because we led with
shadow. §8 wellbeing rule already says "validation before revelation" — the prototype violated it.
Wibawa ×2 should open: "Kamu punya wibawa alami, orang naruh tanggung jawab ke kamu karena mereka
percaya" THEN the cost. And favorable-element must cash out EVERY element it names (Wood AND Fire).

## ROOT CLUSTER 5 — The recognition misses (accuracy doubt)

Two moments he didn't see himself, which threatens the "ini gw banget" core:
- "much water = sensitive person. Not really me, I see myself as nonchalant."
- "gampang nyalain dia — generic."

This is the real risk of high density: more specific claims = more surfaces to disagree with. BUT —
note his nonchalance read: a Water-flooded person who presents as nonchalant is CLASSIC (deep water
looks still on the surface; he even used "nonchalant" which is the Samudra/Hujan calm-surface
signature). We had the answer and didn't use it.

FIX — **pre-empt the obvious self-misread.** Where a mechanic commonly presents as its opposite, say
so: "Anehnya, air sebanyak ini sering keliatan dari luar malah kalem, cuek, susah ditebak — padahal
di dalam nyerep semua." That converts a "that's not me" into a "...oh, actually, yeah." Turns the
density risk into the density payoff.

## ROOT CLUSTER 6 — Funnel / flow friction (real, fixable)

- **"What if I want to check compatibility directly from the front door — I already read mine?"** —
  a returning user has no fast path to a new pairing. Big miss for the household-loop LTV.
- **"Why does it assume my mom?"** — the 3-way upsell hardcodes "Ibu." Presumptuous. Use neutral
  "orang lain" / let them pick the relationship.
- **"maybe 'terdekat' not 'sayang'"** — minor, his call; "sayang" is fine, "orang terdekat" is safer.
- **"is this serious?" (front door)** — the ultra-casual tone at the FIRST impression created doubt
  about legitimacy before he'd seen any value. (See voice note below — this cuts against pure-casual.)
- Payment screen: "pretty legit" — GOOD. QRIS/ewallet/bank reads trustworthy. Keep.

FIX — add a persistent "cek kecocokan" entry for anyone who's read their mirror (front door secondary
CTA + end-of-mirror). Neutralize the 3-way relationship assumption. Decide the "sayang/terdekat" call.

## THE VOICE DECISION — professional, Apple-marketing standard, everywhere

DECIDED: drop the casual "old-friend" register entirely. One voice across the whole product:
professional, composed, precise — Apple-marketing standard. This resolves the "is this serious?"
doubt at the root (that reaction was caused BY the casual front door) and aligns with the §8 voice
lock (2026-06-19) that already moved the brand off casual for credibility at the trust/payment moment.

**What "Apple standard" means for Katon (precise spec, not a vibe):**
Apple's discipline is STRUCTURAL, and that's what we import — NOT cold minimalism. The gold standard
(`matahari-balanced-hubungan-FINAL`) stays the warmth reference. The synthesis:
1. **Short, declarative sentences.** Confidence through brevity. State it, stop. Let it land.
2. **Concrete over abstract, always.** "Air mengelilingi apimu." NOT "ada ketidakseimbangan elemen."
   Apple never says "advanced technology"; it says what the thing does.
3. **Plain words, elevated by restraint.** Accessible but never casual. No prose slang
   (ngerasa/bikin/kayak/capek). No chat particles (tuh/lho/deh). Also not bureaucratic-baku.
4. **Warmth through PRECISION, not chattiness.** The care shows in how exactly it sees her.
   Keep "kamu". Keep the gold standard's humanity; cut its sentence LENGTH by half.
5. **Benefit-forward, no condescension, no hedging.** No "mungkin sedikit", no over-explaining.
6. **Keep the §8 founder profile** (consequence-first, minimal connectives, plain concrete verbs,
   no metaphor that needs decoding). Apple standard = that profile with polish up and length down.

CALIBRATION NOTE: the gold standard is warm, flowing, literary — LONGER than Apple. We are not
discarding it; we are tightening it. Think "the gold standard, but every sentence earns its place and
nothing runs long." Composed authority, not minimalist coldness.

CRITICAL INTERACTION WITH COHERENCE: the Apple voice makes fixing coherence MORE urgent, not less.
Terse confident copy has nowhere to hide a contradiction. "Apimu lemah." stated clean and then
contradicted 3 blocks later reads WORSE than the chatty version, because the crisp delivery earned
more trust before the break. Professional voice raises the stakes on every coherence fix below.

## PRIORITY ORDER (what to fix first)

1. **The coherence spine** (Cluster 2) — the DM-isn't-the-tallest-bar explanation + one-villain
   through-line + no-surprise-mechanics. Without this, nothing else matters; he stops trusting.
2. **Name-it/close-it** (Cluster 1) — weld every mechanism to its payoff in the same block.
3. **Both-faces + handle** (Cluster 4) — gift before cost, always an actionable. Fixes "no positivity."
4. **Jargon glosses + yin/yang fix** (Cluster 3).
5. **Pre-empt self-misreads** (Cluster 5).
6. **Funnel entries + relationship neutrality** (Cluster 6).
7. **Voice: professional Apple-standard throughout** (no zoning — one composed voice everywhere).

The strategy is validated. He was engaged enough to catch our mistakes. Now make the engine as
coherent as he is.
