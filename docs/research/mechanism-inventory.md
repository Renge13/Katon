## PART 3 — DOES THE ARCHETYPE SURVIVE?

Short answer: **yes, but demoted from "the reading" to "the wrapper."**

The archetype was carrying two jobs it can't both do well:
- **Job 1 — the shareable identity object** (the sharecard name, "Hujan yang Tak Reda"). Here it is
  irreplaceable. A named, watercolor-able, comparable-with-friends identity is the virality surface.
  Ten Gods badges ("Zuo Guan") do NOT share — nobody posts "I'm Sitting on Officer." **Keep the
  archetype for this. It is the product's face.**
- **Job 2 — the depth of the reading body.** Here it FAILS, and that failure is the hamster wheel.
  One archetype = one driver = one fact, padded to seven beats. The archetype cannot generate
  fact-density because it *is* a single fact.

The fix is a division of labor the whole inventory points to:

> **Archetype = the FACE (card, name, one-paragraph portrait, shareable).**
> **The mechanism stack = the BODY (a sequence of distinct facts, each stated once).**

The reading body stops being "the archetype, explained at length" and becomes "here are 5–7 true
things about your specific chart, each a different fact." The archetype opens it and the card carries
it into the world; the facts fill it. This is exactly Gemini's structure — a memorable frame
(nothing, actually — that's Gemini's *weakness*) followed by stacked distinct facts. **Katon can beat
Gemini precisely by keeping the shareable archetype face that Gemini lacks AND adopting the fact-stack
body that Gemini has.** That's a combination neither the current Katon nor Gemini has.

### What this does to the 5 states
The state (B2) stops being the *headline personalization* and becomes *one fact among several* — a
strong one (it's still the dominant-imbalance story), but it no longer has to carry the whole reading
alone. That directly dissolves your founder-flagged "samey" problem: the six Balanced cells felt
identical because Balanced + shared skeleton = the same single driver every time. Once the body is a
fact-stack, two Balanced people **diverge** because their strength badge, seat, missing element, and
branch-clashes differ even when the state matches. **The variation you were trying to author into the
prose is already sitting in the chart, unused.**

## PART 4 — THE PRINCIPLE, RESTATED FOR THE REWRITE

Your instinct is correct and the inventory confirms it: **the experience is fact-exposure, not prose.**

- Every beat must deliver a NEW chart fact, not a new sentence about an old one.
- A fact stated once, plainly, beats the same fact rotated through seven metaphors.
- The flatter/depth comes from *quantity of true specific claims*, not depth of any one.
- Mechanism is not the spoiler to be hidden (the current "river withholds the driver" rule) —
  Gemini proves **mechanism stated openly IS the engagement** ("Fire evaporates your Water"). The
  withhold-the-driver rule was solving for a single-fact reading where the one driver was all you
  had to sell. With a fact-stack you have plenty to give away up front and still more behind the pay
  gate. **Reconsider the withhold rule** — it may be actively suppressing the thing that engages.

### The cheapest-to-most-expensive build order (all pre-Level-3):
1. Surface B3 (strength badge) + B4/B5 (dominant/missing as facts) — pure surfacing, content already implied.
2. Add E1–E5 branch-interaction block (incl. flattering absence) — boolean logic + ~5 short authored blocks per case.
3. Add C3/D3 seat badge — one Ten-God lookup table + per-god short blocks.
4. Add B6 favorable-element guidance as an explicit actionable beat.
5. (Phase 2) F3 dated trigger — annual tier + return mechanic.

Everything above is buildable without opening full Ten Gods (D1/D4). The Level-3 gate stays shut; you
were never actually blocked on it. You were blocked on *surfacing the facts you already compute.*

## PART 5 — THE ONE STRATEGIC RISK TO DECIDE BEFORE BUILDING

Coherence. You removed the branch layer (E) from the card because mixing element-axis and branch-axis
made the card incoherent. That reasoning is real and it will resurface: a fact-stack body mixes
axes by design (element strength + Ten God + branch clash live in different "languages").

Gemini's reading is, in fact, **mildly incoherent** — it jumps between strength-talk, seat-talk, and
clash-talk with no unifying thread. It got away with it because each fact was individually engaging
and users forgive incoherence when every beat tells them something new about themselves.

So the decision is explicit: **are you willing to trade top-to-bottom coherence for fact-density in
the reading body?** The card stays coherent (element axis only, archetype face). The *body* becomes a
fact-stack that is less unified but far more engaging. My read: yes, take the trade — but do it
knowingly, and use the archetype as the thin connective tissue ("all of these are ways your [Hujan]
nature shows up") so the stack has at least a nominal spine. That gives you Gemini's density WITH a
coherence thread Gemini never had.
