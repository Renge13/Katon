This skill is the foundation of every reading. Keep it current by rule, not by memory:
1. **When a new reading passes a COLD READ:** deepen that archetype's entry (§3) with the validated
   lived behavior the reading proved, and promote that domain's lens (§7) from PROVISIONAL to LOCKED.
2. **When any locked decision changes** (names, thresholds, voice): update here the same day, and bump
   the version. Do not let the addendum and the skill disagree.
3. **Mark provenance:** every archetype entry and domain lens is tagged [LOCKED: cold-read validated],
   [DRAFT: written not validated], or [LEAN: identity sketch only]. Never silently upgrade a tag.
4. **The gold standard (§9) is the bar.** If a new reading beats it, update the reference.

## §1. Target User
**Primary (pays):** Indonesian woman, mid-20s to 40s, real adult stakes (career, relationships,
money, family pressure). Not buying information; buying permission to act on a decision she's leaning
toward, or relief from one she's stuck on. The reading must SEE her, then point that seeing at her
live question.
**Secondary (shares):** Indonesian Gen Z, 15-25. Shares because the result is identity-affirming and
looks good. Doesn't need to believe in BaZi; shares what feels true about her.

## §2. Personalization = archetype × element-STATE (the moat)
Day-Master-alone produces a STEREOTYPE wrong for most people. Personalization happens at
**archetype × element-state**. Five states (full mechanics in companion doc + §4):
Governed / Depleted / Amplified / Over-fueled / Balanced. The state SELECTS which pre-written variant
applies; it does not generate. Deterministic: same birthdate (+ optional hour) → same state → same
reading. THIS IS THE PRODUCT. Non-negotiable.

## §3. The 10 Day Master Archetypes (canonical names LOCKED v5.1)
Naming pattern: a grand/substantial base noun + a state-modifier that BENDS it (modifier need not use
"yang"). The element TRUTH is fixed; the modifier carries state/personalization.

**MODIFIER RULE (v5.2, applies to card + every helper prompt):** the state modifier must be an IMAGE
that encodes the element-state, NEVER the literal state name. The picture carries the meaning so the
subject FEELS it; the mechanics word means nothing to her.
- Governed Matahari (Water floods Fire) → "di Balik Awan" (sun behind clouds). NOT "yang Tertekan".
- Balanced Matahari (warm, steady, nothing extreme) → "yang Teduh" (warm but shaded). NOT "yang Seimbang".
"Seimbang / Tertekan / Amplified / Depleted / Over-fueled" are mechanics — banned from any headline.

CANONICAL 10: 甲 Jati · 乙 Akar · 丙 Matahari · 丁 Pelita · 戊 Gunung · 己 Ladang · 庚 Pedang ·

Cahaya kecil tapi tetep, ngeliat yang orang lewatin, dalemnya kaya. Api dari dalam, bukan pinjeman.

Stabil, susah digoyang, dipercaya. Kekuatan: diandelin, sabar, praktis, nampung. Bayangan: susah

## §4. Element-state (compressed; full mechanics in companion doc, thresholds in addendum §7)
Five states, asymmetric thresholds (self-element needs higher bar by construction):
- **Amplified**: self-element ≥ 2.0× avg. The pure/extreme version of the archetype.
- **Governed**: controller-element ≥ 1.5× avg. Self cooled/turned inward/pressured.
- **Over-fueled**: feeder-element ≥ 1.5× avg. Over-resourced, can choke on its fuel.
- **Depleted**: feeder-element ≤ 0.15. Runs on reserves, no fuel source.
- **Balanced**: fallback, ~40% of charts (LARGEST segment). No dominant/missing — the "why" IS the
  balance; do NOT invent a missing element. The hard case to write (no element to point at) — the
  craft is making well-calibrated X feel like an identity, not beige.
Priority: Governed > Depleted > Amplified > Over-fueled > Balanced.
Prevalence (no-hour, real data): Balanced ~40 · Amplified ~24 · Governed ~15 · Over-fueled ~11 · Depleted ~10.
WRITING PRIORITY follows prevalence: Balanced first.

## §5. Feed/Drain (state-aware; full rule in companion doc)
ONE rule drives both the card's "Yang Menenangkan / Yang Melelahkan" zone and the paid "Yang Menenangkan vs
Yang Melelahkan" beat — they MUST agree. Principle: what moves you toward balance feeds you; what
worsens imbalance drains you. Depends on STATE (e.g. Amplified is NOT fed by more of its feeder; it
needs draining — its output or controller). Labels are a fixed system string (§8). Why-lines describe
the EFFECT of those people, never the mechanism (spoil rule, §6).

## §6. The reading: river → ocean

### Free "river" — DESCRIBES the pattern, WITHHOLDS the driver. Maximal recognition, ZERO mechanism.
- **Siapa Kamu** (self in motion, domain-independent): recognition that flatters/intrigues. ~3-4 sent.
- **Kenapa Begini** (domain-independent): the WHY via element balance in PLAIN language (no Chinese
  chars, no missing-element reveal). For Balanced, naming the dominant element is fine (its truth IS
  the balance); for imbalanced states, name only the dominant, NEVER the missing/causal one.
- **Ke Mana Ini Bawa Kamu** (domain-specific consequence): recognition + OPEN LOOP. End on an explicit
  "and you don't know why" so she finishes feeling seen but told an answer exists she doesn't have.
  This is the conversion engine (curiosity gap). NO cause-list, NO signature metaphor here — those are
  paid-beat-exclusive.
- **Bridge** (domain-specific, up to 3 per archetype, sub-option selects): a FORK in HER voice that
  implicates her. Quoted inner voice — natural speech (nggak/kok/kayak OK), exempt from brand register.

### Paywall (river-mouth)
Locked accordion of paid section TITLES (must match beat headings §8 exactly) + helper lines promising
UNDERSTANDING/DIRECTION. NEVER blurred real content. ONE CTA Rp 49.000 single domain. Compact 八字
credibility PROMISE; full pillars are the post-payment reveal. WhatsApp only after "Buka".

### Paid "ocean" — 7 beats, emotional DIP (recognition↑ honest-depth↓ strength+direction↑)
1. **Yang Perlu Kamu Dengar Dulu** — shield-down validation. MUST validate something the free read did
   NOT say (e.g. the unthanked COST of the gift), not restate "you're good at X". ~2-3 sent.
2. **Bagaimana Ini Muncul** — 3 concrete lived scenes (bulleted), BEFORE the reframe.
3. **Yang Sebenarnya Terjadi** — the reframe / hidden driver (withheld from free). Open STRAIGHT into
   the reveal after ≤1 line re-acknowledgment; don't re-summarize her state. Framed as reasonable
   adaptation, never a verdict. The most important beat. Owns the cause-list + signature metaphor.
4. **Yang Menenangkan vs Yang Melelahkan** — state-aware feed/drain made actionable (who, by
   archetype). MUST agree with the card.
5. **Empat Pilarmu · 八字** — pillars + element bars (post-pay proof). No-hour: [static: hourExplanation].
6. **Cara Memutuskannya** — concrete archetype-state decision rule (highlighted one-line rule, in her
   inner voice). Returns agency.
7. **Apa Artinya** — what a yes/no each MEANS (not a rhetorical question). Leaves hope + agency.
Closer: [static: closer]. Then other 2 domains as warm upsell.

## §7. DOMAIN LENSES (what each domain reading is ABOUT)

### Hubungan [LOCKED: multiple readings felt-test passed]
Her relationships — romantic, family, the give/take patterns. The live decision is usually about a
specific person (stay/leave, why-do-I-feel-X-with-them, am-I-too-much/too-little). Safest domain.

### Karier [DRAFT: one reading written (Pedang Balanced), cold-read pending]
Her work and ambition. NOT job-hunting tactics. About her relationship to work: how she leads,
decides, is valued or drained, handles uncertainty she can't control. Bridge owns the specific
situation (waiting/deciding/leaving); body is career-universal (addendum §9a).

### Uang [DRAFT: writing first reading now (Permata Amplified)]
Her relationship with MONEY — earning style, spending values, what security means to her, her
financial decision-pattern, her money-fears. Money-PSYCHOLOGY, not prophecy or advice.
**HARD GUARDRAILS (Uang is the highest-risk domain):**
- NEVER predict fortune ("rezekimu akan lancar/seret"). This is fortune-telling — violates "ini bukan
  ramalan" and the Muslim-market sensitivity. More dangerous in Uang than any other domain.
- NEVER give financial advice ("sebaiknya nabung/investasi/jual"). Not licensed, not positioned.
- NEVER induce money-shame or imply she's bad with money / will be poor. Wellbeing rule.
- DO: read how she RELATES to money as an extension of her character (a Permata values quality over
  quantity; a Ladang gives money away; a Pedang decides fast and judges the call later). The reading
  reflects her money-psychology back to her, and points it at her live money-question via the bridge.
- The reframe lives in money-as-mirror, never money-as-future.

### Keluarga — REJECTED as separate domain. Family stress collapses into Uang for this demographic.

## §8. VOICE + PROSE RULES (the brand's writing — load-bearing)

### Register: ONE voice — polished baku ["rapi tapi hidup", v5.2 redefined]
**[VOICE LOCK CHANGED 2026-06-19 — supersedes the casual "old friend" register, MEMORY decision #4.]**
A thoughtful, articulate writer addressing you directly. Proper, well-built Indonesian (baku) sentences;
always "kamu"; warm but composed — authority that sees you, not a buddy texting you. NO prose slang
(ngerasa/bikin/kayak/capek/rame/nyimpen banned in brand prose). NO chat particles (tuh/lho/deh/yaudah).
Avoid literary excess / purple-prose verbs (menyergap, dicekam, "mencicipi rasa sepi yang paling sunyi"):
baku must stay clean and legible, never ornate. The texture is the current Matahari-Balanced-Hubungan
reading. Sharecard = the compressed end of the same voice.
EXEMPTION (unchanged): quoted inner voice (bridge, beat-6 question) stays natural conversational speech —
it mimics the USER's voice, not the brand's. Real people do not think in baku.
RATIONALE for the change: baku reads as credible/authoritative at the Rp 49K trust moment and presents
one consistent brand register across card + reading. Trade-off accepted: less buddy-intimacy in exchange
for composed authority. The "ini gw banget" recognition now comes from accuracy of the pattern, not from
casual diction.

### Founder voice profile (HOW the brand writes — from line-edit pass)
1. **Consequence-first ordering.** Lead with the observable result / how she's misread; reason trails
   as the reveal. NOT reason-then-result.
2. **Minimal connective scaffolding.** Cut "Maka", "Di situ", "Justru karena itu" as openers. Loose
   join or hard stop reads more natural in Indonesian. (Most consistent founder note.)
3. **Vary rhythm; use fragment + rhetorical question.** "Ada masalah ruwet? Kamu akan temukan ujungnya."
4. **Contrast by CONCESSION, not negation.** "Bisa jadi orang menganggapmu dingin, padahal sebenarnya..."
   NOT "bukan X, tapi Y".
5. **Plain concrete verbs over literary ones.** "menyiksa" not "menguras"; full "menunggu" not clipped.
6. **No metaphor that needs decoding.** Must land on first read. Killed: "tahu bentuknya sendiri",
   "garis untuk turun". Kept: "pedang terhunus yang tidak menemukan apa pun untuk dipotong".

### Anti-AI prose rules (HARD — same tier as no-em-dash)
1. No "bukan X, tapi/melainkan/justru Y" as a sentence skeleton (the #1 AI tell). Keep reframe MEANING,
   kill the repeated SHAPE.
2. Prefer positive constructions over stacked negatives. Say what a thing IS.
3. No literal English-idiom translation ("clear cut" → "hitam dan putih"; "feel alive" → "paling tajam").
4. Causal cohesion: consecutive sentences connect; strength and vulnerability are the SAME trait seen
   from two sides — show the link.

### Hard locks
- NO em-dash (—). Banned words: "cetak biru" (use "bentuk dasarmu"), mystical set (alam semesta/energi/
  takdir/ramalan/aura). "nguras"/"ngisi" only as body verbs.
- Feed/drain labels (system string, identical everywhere): card + paid section + paywall title
  "Yang Menenangkan vs Yang Melelahkan". [v5.2: replaces the inconsistent BIKIN TENANG/CAPEK (interp) and
  BIKIN TENANG/PANAS (card+states) — one string now, baku, brand-consistent.]
- Beat headings (paywall accordion titles MUST match exactly): 1 Yang Perlu Kamu Dengar Dulu ·
  2 Bagaimana Ini Muncul · 3 Yang Sebenarnya Terjadi · 4 Yang Menenangkan vs Yang Melelahkan ·
  5 Empat Pilarmu · 八字 · 6 Cara Memutuskannya · 7 Apa Artinya.
- Pronoun split: sharecard = no subject pronoun; reading = "kamu".
- Never close a door / no fatalistic conclusions. Insight opens; verdict is banned.

### Wellbeing
Written for an intelligent woman with real problems. No clichés ("kamu cantik dan mandiri!"). Validation
before revelation. Name a behavior, let her supply the feeling. A verdict that leaves no agency is BANNED.
