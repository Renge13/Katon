## 1. THE DAY MASTER — why it is the center (for onboarding + education copy)

The Day Master (日主) is the Heavenly Stem of your Day pillar — the one character, of the eight, that
is *you*. The other seven are the world around that self.

Everything centers on it because the other characters have no fixed meaning alone. A Fire character
is only "wealth" or "pressure" or "creativity" *relative to who you are*. Change the Day Master and
the same Fire means something entirely different. So the Day Master is the LENS that turns a grid of
elements into a reading about a person.

Classical analogy worth using in onboarding: the Day Master is the CEO; the Ten Gods are the
departments (finance, ops, legal, R&D). You cannot know what "finance" means for this company until
you know who runs it.

**Product consequence:** the archetype (= the Day Master) SURVIVES as the spine and the shareable
face. It is not one fact among many — it is the reference frame that makes the other facts legible.
This is the coherence thread. It is classical, not invented.

## 2. THE TEN GODS (十神) — the real relational layer

NOT deities. "Ten Gods" is a traditional name for ten TYPES OF RELATIONSHIP between you and every
other element in the chart. Standard sources are explicit: they are behavior/role patterns, not
supernatural abilities.

Derivation: for any other element, ask — same as me / I produce it / it produces me / I control it /
it controls me = 5 categories. Split each by polarity (same vs opposite yin-yang) = 10.

| # | Chinese | Common name | Relationship | Plain role (Katon-facing seed) |
|---|---------|-------------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | 比肩 | Companion | same element, same polarity | your own drive, self-reliance, peers/allies |
| 2 | 劫财 | Rob Wealth | same element, opp polarity | rivals, competitiveness, boldness, risk |
| 3 | 食神 | Eating God | you produce, same polarity | easy creativity, enjoyment, gentle expression |
| 4 | 伤官 | Hurting Officer | you produce, opp polarity | brilliant, rule-breaking, performative expression |
| 5 | 偏财 | Indirect Wealth | you control, same polarity | windfalls, opportunism, generosity, deal-making |
| 6 | 正财 | Direct Wealth | you control, opp polarity | steady income, thrift, the reliable provider |
| 7 | 七杀 | Seven Killings | controls you, same polarity | raw pressure, discipline under fire, power |
| 8 | 正官 | Direct Officer | controls you, opp polarity | status, duty, structure, reputation, self-control |
| 9 | 偏印 | Indirect Resource| produces you, same polarity | unconventional knowledge, intuition, outsider mind |
| 10| 正印 | Direct Resource | produces you, opp polarity | nurture, education, protection, scholar/mother |

These are the relational vocabulary of the entire reading. Career, money, marriage, authority,
creativity — every life theme is one of these ten roles playing out in a pillar.

**IMPORTANT reframe (from the household-signal user who arrived with the "4 favorable gods = blessed
birthright" caste idea):** do NOT rank the Ten Gods as good/bad or blessed/cursed. Standard practice
is explicit that each has strengths and challenges; none is a "lack" of a trait. Katon presents them
as neutral roles, never a hierarchy. (This was already a locked ethics decision — it holds.)

## 3. THE FOUR PILLARS ARE THE DOMAINS (this dissolves the input-domain choice)

Standard positional mapping:
| Pillar | Life domain | Time period | Katon use |
|--------|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| Year | ancestry, social face, early environment | childhood ~0–15 | context beat |
| Month | **career, parents, "the season you were born into"** | ~15–30, MOST POWERFUL | career reading + drives DM strength |
| Day (branch) | **core self + marriage / partner** (Spouse Palace) | the self | relationship reading (north-star axis) |
| Hour | **children, ambitions, private inner world, late life** | later years | UNLOCKED BY BIRTH HOUR |

Consequences:
- **Kill the upfront domain choice.** The chart already assigns Hubungan (Day branch), Karier (Month),
  legacy/children (Hour) positionally. Asking the user to pick a domain the chart already maps was
  redundant friction. Domain depth becomes a READING SECTION, not an input gate.
- **Birth-hour prompt is now a TRUE unlock, not a nag.** "Know your birth hour? It reveals your Hour
  pillar — your children, your ambitions, your private inner world. Without it, this quarter of your
  chart stays dark." This is honest: the Hour pillar is a real, missing 1/4 of the chart. Frame as
  invitation to completeness, never as error.
- The Month branch also determines the SEASON you were born into, which directly sets Day Master
  strength ("born in scorching summer, your Water evaporates"). This is the engaging strength-mechanism
  Gemini used and it lives in the Month pillar.

## 4. STRENGTH (强/弱) — the discarded badge, now surfaced

Standard, universal step: is the Day Master strong or weak? Determined by how much support (same
element + resource) vs drain (output + wealth + pressure) it receives, with the birth SEASON
(month branch) weighted heavily.

- Strong DM benefits from being drained/challenged (output, wealth, power elements help).
- Weak DM benefits from support (resource, companion elements help).
- Strength is NOT good/bad — it is context. State this explicitly (defuses the caste anxiety).

This single badge ("Your Day Master is Weak / Strong / Balanced") is the cheapest high-engagement
fact in the system. Katon already computes it inside state-resolution and throws it away. Surface it.

The FAVORABLE ELEMENT (用神) falls straight out of strength: it is what the chart needs to balance.
This is the actionable conclusion users pay attention to ("lean on Metal and Water — collaborate,
don't carry alone"). It is also the real mechanism under the feed/drain compatibility layer — so
compatibility and the favorable-element beat are ONE computation, consistent by construction.

## 5. BRANCH RELATIONS — the clash/harm layer (restored to the READING, not the card)

Relationships between the branches. All are cheap boolean pairwise checks on data already computed.
| Relation | Chinese | Meaning | Note |
|----------|---------|---------|------|
| Clash | 冲 | violent axis / total opposition between two life-domains | ABSENCE is a flattering, engaging fact too |
| Harm | 害 | quiet drain between two domains | e.g. career quietly encroaching on home |
| Combination | 合 | fusion / pull between two domains | can lock or transform an element |
| Punishment | 刑 | self-undermining friction | subtle internal pattern |
| Destruction | 破 | minor ongoing adjustment | low-weight |

Two rules from the earlier analysis, now settled:
- This was correctly REMOVED FROM THE CARD (mixing branch-axis with element-axis made the card
  incoherent). That decision stands for the CARD.
- It should live in the DEEP READING as its own fact block. The competitor's most engaging passages
  were here — including the flattering ABSENCE case ("you have NO clash, here's what that stability
  means") and the DATED TRIGGER ("the next Goat year, 2027, temporarily clashes your Day pillar").

## 6. THE TIME LAYER — Luck Pillars (大运) + Annual (流年)

Standard and essential: the birth chart is the static foundation; Luck Pillars are 10-year cycles
that overlay it, and each Annual year interacts too. Much of a practitioner's PRACTICAL reading is
"how does your chart respond to the current cycle."

- **Current Luck Pillar** = the "era" you're in now (age-dependent, needs birth year + gender +
  month pillar to compute start age and direction).
- **Annual + dated trigger** = "2027 Goat clashes your Ox" — a specific, dated, personal,
  falsifiable-feeling event. The single most engaging line the competitor produced.

Product role: this is BOTH the renewable annual tier AND the "return mechanic" in the north star
(the reason she comes back at life's turning points). Frame as WEATHER/SEASONS, never prophecy —
a locked ethics line that holds.

## 7. SHARECARD REVAMP — remove the prose, pack the mechanics

Current card carries paragraphs of prose (dimensions "Surface→Turn", flowery why-lines). That prose
is the same hamster-wheel problem in miniature, and it makes every card read as a fill-in-the-blank
template. **Strip the prose. The card becomes a DENSE FACT BADGE-SHEET** — a chart-at-a-glance that
is instantly comparable between two friends and screenshot-worthy BECAUSE it's information-rich, not
because it's poetic.

Card = the FACE + a compressed fact panel. Proposed contents (all one-liners / labels, zero
paragraphs):
- **Archetype name + element glyph** (the shareable identity — KEEP; it's what people post)
- **Day Master** stated plainly (e.g. "癸 Yin Water") — the real anchor, quietly shown
- **Strength badge** (Strong / Weak / Balanced) — a title people want to compare
- **Element bars** (the 5-count distribution as a tiny bar chart — visual, instant, honest)
- **Dominant + Missing element** flags (one word each)
- **Top Ten God** (their defining role, one label — e.g. "Direct Officer / Duty")
- **Spouse-Palace seat** (one label — the relationship hook)
- **A signature branch fact** where present (e.g. "No Clash — rare structural stability")
- Footer: katon.app

Rules for the card:
- NO paragraphs. Labels, glyphs, bars, one-word flags only.
- Every element on the card is a REAL mechanic (no invented flavor).
- Two friends' cards must be legible side-by-side at a glance (that's the virality mechanic).
- The card TEASES the mechanics; the reading EXPLAINS them. Card = index, reading = body.

The card stops being a poem about you and becomes a *baseball card of your chart*. That is far more
shareable ("look how different our charts are") and far more honest.

## 8. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE — the actual moat

The moat is NOT fewer facts. It is the SAME complete facts, ordered so a layperson can walk in at the
shallow end and wade deeper at their own pace. Deceptively simple surface over the full complex engine.

Ordering principle: **from what people care about most → outward.** Not from what BaZi teaches first.
People care, in rough order: who am I → who do I love / who's right for me → what should I do about
[my live question] → what's coming. Structure the disclosure to that emotional order, then attach the
mechanism that answers each.

Proposed disclosure ladder (each layer = new FACTS, stated once, 1–2 sentences each — never 7 beats):

**Layer 0 — The Card (free, instant, shareable).** The fact badge-sheet above. The hook.

**Layer 1 — The Mirror (free): "Who you are."**
- Day Master identity (the archetype portrait — 1 tight paragraph, not seven).
- Strength badge + what it means in one line.
- Dominant / missing element as plain facts ("flooded with Water, no Fire — here's what that does").
- Top Ten God = your defining drive, one crisp fact.
Each is ONE new true thing. No padding. This alone out-densities the current 7-beat body.

**Layer 2 — The People (free tease → paid depth): "Who fills you, who drains you."**
- Spouse-Palace seat fact (your partner tendency, from the Day branch).
- Favorable-element → compatibility ("you're steadied by Earth types, drained by more Water").
- Paid: the full relational read (this is RELATION, the paid product — now grounded in the seat +
  Ten Gods + favorable element, not a vibe).

**Layer 3 — The Domains (paid): "Your life areas."**
- Career (Month pillar + relevant Ten Gods).
- Money (Wealth gods).
- Each domain is a positional reading, not a re-explanation of the self.

**Layer 4 — The Weather (paid / renewable): "What's moving now and next."**
- Current Luck Pillar (your era).
- Dated triggers (the 2027-Goat-type lines).
- The return mechanic + annual subscription.

**Layer 5 (birth-hour gated): "Your inner world & legacy."**
- Hour pillar: children, ambitions, private self. Only appears if hour is known — the honest unlock.

Every layer answers a question the user actually has, in the order they have it, with real mechanics
underneath. Complexity fully present; approached one plain fact at a time.

## 9. RULE AUDIT — every current rule that now HINDERS the goal

The goal: maximum true-fact density, plainly stated, progressively disclosed. These locked rules were
built for the 2-axis single-driver world and now actively work against it. Verdicts:

**DROP / REVERSE:**

  single driver was the ONLY thing to sell. With a full fact-stack, give mechanism freely in the
  mirror and keep DEPTH (relation, domains, weather) behind the gate. The gate moves from
  "mechanism vs no mechanism" to "self vs self+others+time."
- **"7-beat paid skeleton."** DROP. It exists to pad one driver into seven sections. Replace with a
  fact-stack: N distinct facts, each 1–2 sentences, each a different mechanic. Length follows fact
  count, not a fixed skeleton.
- **"Dose not length / the paid tier is short because it answers a different question."** OBSOLETE.
  Paid is now deep because it has MORE MECHANICS (relation + domains + time), not because it's a
  different flavor of the same fact.
- **"Prune Karier and Uang / domains are the wrong axis."** REVERSE. The pillars ARE domains,
  positionally. Career (Month) and Money (Wealth gods) are real chart layers, not arbitrary slices.
  Keep them as Layer-3 reading sections; just don't make them an INPUT choice.
- **"Card = archetype + state modifier + flattering prose dimensions."** DROP the prose. Card becomes
  the dense fact badge-sheet (§7).
- **"Shio/branch mechanic removed entirely."** PARTIAL REVERSE. Stays off the CARD; RESTORED in the
  deep reading as its own fact block (§5), including the flattering-absence and dated-trigger cases.
- **"Level 3 (Ten Gods) deferred until Level 2 validated insufficient."** DROP the deferral. Ten Gods

- **"Personalization = archetype × element-state (the moat)."** DEMOTE. The state is now ONE fact

**KEEP (still serve the goal):**

**RE-EXAMINE (open, founder's call):**
