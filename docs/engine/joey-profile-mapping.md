## Corrections this confirms

- **"Warrior" = 七殺, NOT 劫財.** Corroborated by charts 6 and 12 which already mapped it that way.
- **"Dir" = 正財 (Director / Direct Wealth), NOT 正官.** "Director" reads like an officer title in English
  and that is the trap. Joey's parenthetical says Direct Wealth.
- **"Leader" = 劫財** (Rob Wealth), which is what "Warrior" was mistakenly assigned.

## Naming reminder

The English column is **Joey Yap's trademarked IP** (`CLAUDE.md` rule 19). It appears here and in test
fixtures only, as the key for reading his published output. It must never reach user-facing copy. The
Katon column is what ships.

## Ties

Joey publishes ties. Chart 9 has 98/98 at the top, chart 13 has 80/80. **Exact rank-order is not
meaningfully assertable on a tied pair** — any Oracle 2 metric must treat tied scores as
order-independent, or it will fail charts that are actually correct.
