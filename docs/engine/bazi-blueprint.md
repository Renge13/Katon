# Katon — Full BaZi Feature Blueprint

**Status:** Living document. Purpose: catalog *everything* BaZi carries — every engine, tier, marker, and derivation — with what each reveals to the reader, an interpretation example, and a **Pull Power** value.

**Methodological stance (locked):** Katon follows the **旺衰法 (strength-balance) school**, consistent with Joey Yap's system, which is our ground-truth validation reference and the tool our users cross-check against. This is one of several valid BaZi traditions; classical 子平法 differs on 用神 derivation and structure determination. We adopt classical rigor **wherever it prevents a false or self-contradictory statement**, and ignore it wherever it is only a lineage preference the consumer never perceives. Correctness and coherence create depth — not orthodoxy.

**Pull Power = cumulative dopamine meter (NOT a flat per-fact score).** Every resonant fact on the free read fills an imaginary meter; crossing a breakpoint psychologically converts the reader to paid. Facts are unequal: some are **PEAK** (wave-makers), some are **BUILD-UP** (setup that makes peaks land). The hierarchy engine therefore *sequences* facts into a narrative arc (dramaturgy), it does not rank them by isolated score. The 1–5 tags below indicate peak-potential, not standalone value.

**Framing law (applies to every feature):** *Blueprint, not biography. Pattern you carry, not fate you're sentenced to* — for the FREE mirror. The PAID tier uses **predictive recognition with agency** (see locked decisions): it names a specific energy, its default tendency, and the agency point — aggressive and precise, never blunt-binary fatalism ("2026 is bad"), which is falsifiable and a scale/trust risk.

**FREE / PAID SPLIT (locked):**
- **FREE = the Mirror.** Everything about the SELF from a single chart — deep, specific, a complete valuable product on its own. This is the acquisition + meter-filling engine. It is NOT held shallow to "save content for paid."
- **PAID = You vs the External.** What a solo chart structurally cannot answer: (1) **Compatibility** (you × another person), (2) **Time-bound decisions** (the timing/seasons layer — "when does this shift"), and future relation axes (child, parent). The paid peak is the dimension the mirror cannot contain.

## HOW THE ENGINE FANS OUT (read this first)

BaZi is not 50 independent lookups. It's ~8 **core engine facts**, each **branching** into many concrete reveals. Understanding this is the key to "fact after fact without verbosity" — you compute a few things, then derive many.

```
Birth date (+ hour if known)
        │
        ▼
   4 PILLARS (8 characters)  ── the raw chart
        │
        ├── DAY MASTER ─────────► elemental temperament (the "what you're made of")
        │
        ├── MONTH BRANCH ──────► Ten God PROFILE (the headline "who you are")
        │                        └► seasonal command → feeds strength
        │
        ├── STRENGTH VERDICT ──► strong / weak / follow (the "what fuels you")
        │        │
        │        └──► FAVORABLE ELEMENT (用神) ──► fans out into:
        │                   ├── lucky COLORS
        │                   ├── lucky DIRECTION
        │                   ├── supportive SEASON
        │                   ├── favorable CAREER fields
        │                   ├── favorable MATERIALS / numbers
        │                   └── who/what DRAINS you (unfavorable element)
        │
        ├── TEN GODS (all 8 chars) ─► roles, relationships, drives (texture)
        │
        ├── SYMBOLIC STARS (神煞) ──► ~30 usable trait-markers (the "DND feats")
        │
        ├── PILLAR INTERACTIONS ───► clashes / combinations / harms (life-area tension)
        │
        ├── THE PALACES ───────────► maps each pillar to a life domain (family/spouse/career/children)
        │
        └── TIME LAYER (大運/流年) ─► Luck Pillars + Annual (the "campaign timeline")
```

**Implication:** the free "mirror" can be *deep* using only the static branches (everything above the TIME LAYER). The TIME LAYER is a separate, optional axis.

## LOCKED COHERENCE RULES (interpretation-layer requirements)

These are engine/copy rules that prevent self-contradiction — Katon's #1 failure mode. Coherence break = reader silently feels "this is random" and closes the tab. All derived from adversarial review.

**CR-1 — Profile vs Favorable-Element tension → name it as identity.**
When the Profile's element is *unfavorable* to the Day Master, do NOT state profile-praise and element-avoidance flatly. Once Katon's legitimacy-anchor sentence names the element behind the profile (which specificity pushes us to do), the two collide visibly. Resolution is not to hide the tension but to name it as a truth about her: *"Your gift and your cost are the same current."* Real people feel their greatest strength is also their wound — handled, the contradiction becomes a PEAK-pull line. (Note: invisible to a lay reader UNTIL Katon explains the mechanism; it is the explanation that exposes it, not her decoding hanzi. So the rule activates specifically in element-explained/high-specificity copy.)
**TRIGGER CONDITION (must not fire on every chart, or it becomes manufactured drama/noise):** fire the tension frame ONLY when (a) the Profile's element is unfavorable to the Day Master, OR (b) the Profile's Ten God functionally contradicts the strength verdict (e.g. a strong chart whose Profile is a nourishing/Resource god that would overfeed it). On harmonious charts (profile element favorable AND aligned with the verdict), state it straight — no forced tension.

**CR-2 — The "loud alternative Ten God" check (anti-mistype).**
The single headline Profile (Month-Branch structural) can mismatch the lived self when a *different* Ten God is heavily rooted/revealed across the other pillars. Opening "You bring order and structure" to a woman whose chart screams The Creator = instant specificity failure ("this isn't me"), MBTI-mistype syndrome. Engine must tally Ten God dominance across all 8 characters; if a non-profile god is loud, the reading names the internal argument: *"Your structure is The Steward, but you carry a heavy current of The Creator — the tension between building systems and wanting to burn them down. Your chart is an argument between these two forces."* More specific than one profile, and peak-pull because people ARE internal arguments. (Highest-value catch from review.)
**SCORING (must be the SAME tally that drives the visual element/Ten-God bars — a bar showing moderate X while the text calls X "loud" is itself a coherence break):** weighted Ten God tally across all 8 characters — stem appearance heavier than branch main-qi heavier than residual hidden stem (align exact weights with the Track B strength-tally already specced; do not invent a second scoring). "Loud alternative" fires when a non-Profile Ten God's score is within a calibratable margin of the Profile's score (margin = a tunable constant, set during validation, NOT hardcoded blind). One threshold constant, calibrated — not a coin flip.

**CR-3 — Missing Element must be tagged favorable / unfavorable / neutral.**
Never deliver a missing-element reveal in a single emotional register. A missing *favorable* element = "something you seek, it restores you." A missing *unfavorable* element = "your chart is protected from [its downside]." A missing *neutral* element = "this area runs quiet for you." Otherwise weak-Fire ("you lack Water" — good, she should want it) and weak-Earth ("you lack Wood" — good, it would drain her) get identical mournful copy, contradicting the strength advice. Frame as natal starting-hand, not permanent lack.

**CR-4 — Career/lifestyle advice = Favorable Element × Profile, never element alone.**
"Water = trade/media/logistics" is so broad every Water person gets it → specificity failure. Cross the favorable element WITH the Profile Ten God: Water+Steward ≠ Water+Dazzler.

**CR-5 — "Weak" is banned as consumer terminology.**
"Your Fire is weak" lands as personal inadequacy before the explanation arrives. Use: *lean, adaptive, context-driven, draws power from surroundings.* Strength is a fuel model, never a verdict on worth.

**CR-6 — Symbolic stars must carry Palace context.**
"You have Peach Blossom" is generic. "Peach Blossom sits in your Hour pillar — your private self has a magnetism others don't see at first" is specific. Every star reads through the life-domain of the pillar it anchors to.

## LOCKED CALCULATION-ANCHOR RULES (engine correctness — real bugs, adopting)

Wrong marker = false statement about the person = coherence break. These are not school-preferences; they are right/wrong.

- **Symbolic stars have specific anchor formulas, not "flags on a pillar."** Peach Blossom keyed off Year/Day Branch via trine group (申子辰→酉, 寅午戌→卯, 巳酉丑→午, 亥卯未→子); Nobleman off Day/Year Stem; Intelligence (文昌) off Day Stem; Sky Horse off Year/Day Branch. Two people with same Day Stem but different Year Branch can have different stars. The star catalog must carry each exact anchor.
- **Yang Blade (羊刃) exists ONLY for Yang stems:** 甲→卯, 丙/戊→午, 庚→酉, 壬→子. The five Yin stems (乙丁己辛癸) have none. Checking it universally fails on half of charts.
- **12 Life Stages are computed from the Day Master against each branch** — not intrinsic to the branch. Same branch = different stage for different Day Masters.
- **Void (空亡)** is derived from the Day pillar's 10-day cycle and applies at the **branch** level (read through the pillar's domain).

## PAID-TIER VOICE (locked): Predictive Recognition with Agency

The paid tier predicts — that is what people pay BaZi for, and Katon's articulation edge lets it predict better than a street reader. But it predicts with a **mechanism and an exit**, never blunt binary fatalism.

- **Rejected (blunt fatalism):** "2026 is a bad year for your marriage." Falsifiable, binary, generates public "scam" posts when it doesn't happen. The street reader survives this on no-memory single visits; a scaled, screenshotted app cannot.
- **Locked (predictive recognition + agency):** name the specific energy, its default tendency, AND the agency point. *"2026 activates a clash in your partnership palace — the old contract breaks. The default drift is silent friction or separation; but this same energy clears space to renegotiate. The people who meet it deliberately reshape the terms; the people who ignore it drift."* Aggressive, specific, shakes — and validates on whether she *acted*, not on a binary event, so it can't be publicly falsified.
- The competitive edge is **precision about the mechanism**, not audacity of prophecy. Depth of recognition deepens on re-read; binary prediction erodes.

## FREE-READ NARRATIVE ARC (dramaturgy, not a list)

The free mirror is a story that fills the meter, not a feature dump. Rough act structure (arrangement stays free per the renderer principle — this is the beat logic, not a template):
1. **Setup** — Day Master + Profile ("who you are").
2. **Rising action** — Strength (as fuel) + Favorable Element + Missing Element ("what you need"), each tagged per CR-3.
3. **Peak + open loop** — a Symbolic Star in its Palace, or a key Interaction (clash in a life-domain), that names a live tension — then hands partial resolution (self-knowledge + one actionable thing) while leaving the *timing* dimension open. Curiosity converts; anxiety does not. She must leave with a complete, valuable read that is missing exactly one dimension: **when it changes** (paid time layer) or **who it's with** (paid compatibility).
