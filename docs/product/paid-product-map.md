# Katon — Paid Product Map

## THE ORGANISING INSIGHT

Your locked strategy already says it: **the axis of desire is people, not life domains.** Users want
themselves seen through the people they love. So the paid catalogue sorts into four families, and
only one of them is about domains.

```
A. SELF          the mirror                          FREE. the acquisition engine.
B. RELATIONAL    you + one other person              the emotional core
C. TEMPORAL      you + time                          the recurring revenue
D. ARTIFACT      the thing you keep or give away     the impulse tier
```

**The finding that should change your sequencing: the strength engine is not the compatibility tax.
It is the gate on almost the entire catalogue.** Compat, every temporal product, and career depth all
need the favorable element. Build it once, unlock four revenue lines.

## 1. COMPATIBILITY — confirmed

Already specced in KATON-compatibility-reading-spec.md. Day Master × Day Master, Fondasi Pasangan
comparison as centrepiece, Ten Aspects fit, Luck Pillar sync. Paywall after the second birthdate is
entered and the pairing tease is seen.

Why it also drives distribution: it structurally **requires a second person**, so every purchase is a
conversation with someone who is not yet a user. It is the only product that markets itself.

## 2. ANNUAL READING — the one you are missing

**This is the single largest recurring product in the entire Chinese metaphysics market.** Every
practitioner sells it, every year, to the same people. It is the difference between one-time buyers
and an annual customer base.

- **Recurring by construction.** Same user, same product, every year, no new acquisition cost.
- **Seasonal spike.** Demand concentrates Dec–Feb around Chinese New Year. A predictable revenue
  season you can plan content and marketing around.
- **Reuses everything.** The natal computation is already cached. An annual reading is the natal
  chart interacting with one new pillar. Marginal engineering after the strength engine is small.
- **Highest willingness to pay per unit**, because it is a ritual purchase, not an impulse. This is
  where your original 80–99k instinct probably actually belongs — not on compatibility.

**Ethics gate is the hardest here.** This is the product that sits closest to fortune-telling and it
collides directly with your locked "no fatalism, no dated prophecy" rule. Your docs already have the
answer: frame it as **cuaca**, never *ramalan*. Name the weather and the agency pivot, never the
outcome. If the ethics framing cannot hold, do not ship it — but it can, and your spec already says how.

---

## 3. PARENT → CHILD — underrated, fits your demographic exactly

Indonesian women 25–40. Many have young children. And this product has properties the romantic one
does not:

- **No shame attached.** Buying a compatibility reading about your marriage can feel like admitting
  doubt. Buying a reading about understanding your child feels like good parenting.
- **Parents spend on children more readily than on themselves.** Universally true, strongly true in
  Indonesia.
- **Repeatable per child**, and the child's chart never expires.
- **Emotionally enormous.** "Why is my child like this" is one of the most searched questions in
  every market on earth.

**But this carries your heaviest ethics load, heavier than 3-way.** You would be narrating a child's
nature to a parent, and the parent may hold that description over the child for years. Hard rules
before it ships: describe tendencies, never fix identity. No predictions about the child's future.
No comparative ranking between siblings. Frame everything as *how to support*, never *what they are*.

Also worth noting: **child → parent** (understanding your mother or father) is the same engine and a
different emotional register — often about reconciliation. Lower priority, same build.

## 4. ARTIFACT TIER — ship with v1

Hi-res card + packaged PDF at ~19k. Already decided in KATON-launch-decisions.md. Same content as the
free reading, better object, plus the chart sheet appendix. **Keepsake psychology, not information
psychology.** Do not put better content behind it or the free mirror becomes a trailer.

**Add: "gift a reading."** Indonesian gift culture is strong, and a gifted reading is viral by
construction — the recipient becomes a user who did not cost you acquisition. Cheapest new SKU you
have; it is the existing product with a different checkout flow and a delivery message.

---

## 5. LUCK PILLAR MAP (大運) — the life-arc product

The 10-year cycles. "Where you are in your arc, what phase just ended, what phase you are in now."
One-time purchase, deep, and it answers a question people genuinely carry: *why did everything change
around age 28.*

Retrospective framing makes it uniquely safe: describing a phase the user has **already lived** is
verifiable and generates enormous trust, and it does it without predicting anything. Lead with the
past, treat the present phase as weather, say nothing dated about the future.

## THE PRICING QUESTION THIS RAISES

You have one real market datapoint: imajidiri charges **Rp15,000** for a self artifact.

That suggests the impulse ceiling is low and your 80–99k instinct was aimed at the wrong product.
Reconsider the ladder:

| Tier | Product | Band | Psychology |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impulse | Card + PDF, gift | 19k | keepsake |
| Considered | Compatibility, child | 25–45k | a relationship decision |
| Ritual | Annual reading | 49–99k | yearly, seasonal, high perceived value |
| Premium | Household 3-way | 99–149k | family-scale, highest LTV |

**Test the compat band before committing.** 25–45k with a real cohort beats 80–99k chosen by
intuition. The annual reading is where the higher number probably lives.
