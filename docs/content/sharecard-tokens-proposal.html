The five missing colour tokens

PROPOSAL, 2026-08-03. Nothing here is written into glossary.json or sharecard-mockups-02.html yet. The five LOCKED triples are copied verbatim from the mockup's :root; the five new ones follow the derivation rule measured off them, printed below. Geometry is the 08-03 ruling: a 3:4 canvas with a 63:88 card object floating on it. Marks for the new five are placeholders. Every number on this page came from sharecard-tokens-measure.mjs, in this same directory (OKLCh + WCAG). Still open as of 2026-08-13: the five proposals below are not approved, and lib/card/tokens.js renders them flagged approved: false so the card can be looked at without the proposal being mistaken for a decision.

1 - The rule the locked five actually follow

Measured, not assumed. Three findings, and I followed all three.

  • Field = the object's colour, and the hue is the element family. Fire H36 (Matahari), Wood H152 (Bambu), Water H246 (Samudra), Metal achromatic C 0.01 (Besi Tempa + Permata). The one complete pair - Metal - shares one hue family and splits on lightness: yang L 0.28, yin L 0.94. So a reader learns "grey = Logam" from two cards, and the missing five must not break that.
  • Ink = the field's own hue, chroma collapsed, lightness pushed to the far end. Hue within 10 degrees of the field (Matahari 22 is the outlier), C 0.00-0.02, L 0.93-0.97 on a dark or mid field and L 0.22 on the one light field (Permata). Never pure white, never pure black.
  • Accent = the field's hue, chroma equal or lower, lightness landing between field and ink. Hue within 9 degrees (Matahari 17), C 0.02-0.08 and always at or below the field's chroma, L 0.71-0.88 above a dark field, L 0.59 below the light one.

Two measured floors that constrain anything new: the weakest ink contrast in the locked set is Matahari at 3.04 and the weakest accent contrast is Matahari at 2.22; the closest two colour fields are Samudra vs Besi Tempa at dE 0.09 (OKLab). I held all five proposals above every one of those: ink 4.21 or better, accent 3.02 or better, and no new pair closer than dE 0.11.

5 - One thing I found while checking that constraint

Card B's gradient rule collides with Api Unggun's flat field. Matahari's Card B gradient in the locked mock is #B33305 -> #701C02 -> #330C00, which measures as one hue (36) walked down in lightness: 0.51, 0.36, 0.22. Api Unggun's proposed field sits at L 0.39 in the same family, so it lands dE 0.03 from Matahari's middle stop - a paid Matahari card and a free Api Unggun card would read as the same colour.

This is not fixable in the token. I tried rotating Api Unggun to the wine side (H 19 instead of 32) and it bought 0.01. The cause is the gradient rule itself: "same hue, deepened to near-black" sweeps through the whole lightness range, so any dark field in that family gets crossed. Fire is the only pair where the yang card is the bright one, so Fire is the only place it bites.

My recommendation, your call: keep Card B's gradient in the field's upper band - field minus 0.08 / 0.16 / 0.24 rather than minus 0.16 / 0.31 / 0.45. Matahari B becomes roughly #E04310 -> #B33305 -> #8A2A04: still visibly richer than the flat card, and it never descends into Api Unggun's band. The 9:16 height, the ghost mark and the appendix band are already doing the "this is the paid one" work; the gradient does not need to run to black to carry it. Not actioned.

7 - Two questions the geometry ruling leaves open

1. What separates the card object from the canvas? "The card floats on the colour field" gives the canvas the archetype colour, but then the card needs an edge or it is invisible. Mocked here as: same field colour on both, card distinguished by a hairline inset and a soft shadow. That keeps the whole 1080x1440 image reading as the archetype colour at thumbnail size, and needs no sixth token. The alternative - a different surface value for the card - would add a fourth colour per archetype and thirty more strings. Recommending the hairline; not locked.

2. The margin arithmetic works out exactly. A 3:4 canvas and a 63:88 card admit one uniform margin: solve (1080-2m)/(1440-2m) = 63/88 and m = 86.4, giving a card of 907 x 1267 inside 1080 x 1440. So the card can be optically centred with an equal margin on all four sides and no ratio compromise, which is a small piece of luck. At 86px the margin is 8 percent of the canvas width. If you want it slimmer than that the margins stop being uniform and the card gets a different top-bottom to left-right gap - worth knowing before you tinker.
